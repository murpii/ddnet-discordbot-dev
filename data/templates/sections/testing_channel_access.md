# Accessing Testing Channels
- To see all channels, add a ✅ reaction to this message
- To see individual testing channels, add a ✅ reaction to the submission message in <#{Channels.TESTING_SUBMIT}> of the map's channel you want to see,
  removing the reaction reverts it
- Find archived channels at https://ddnet.tw/testlogs/
# Becoming a Tester
If you're interested in being a part of the testing team,
- First, test some maps in public channels on your own
- Then, reach out to one of our official testers, and we'll consider you as a Trial Tester
- Trial testers get promoted to official testers after demonstrating their testing capabilities
