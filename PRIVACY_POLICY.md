# Privacy Policy for the DDNet Discord Bot

**Last updated:** June 20, 2026 \
**Contact:** https://ddnet.org/staff/#contact

This policy explains what the DDNet Discord bot ("the bot", "we") does with your
personal data on the DDNet Discord server, why, and what rights you have. It
covers only the bot. Discord's own handling of your data is governed by Discord's
Privacy Policy.

## 1. What we process

- **Discord account data:** your user ID, username, display name, and roles, as
  provided by Discord. Your user ID and username may be stored in our records, for
  example in moderation logs and ticket transcripts.
- **Message content:** the bot reads messages in the server to enforce the rules
  and to run features such as tickets. Messages inside a ticket, including any
  attachments, are saved into a transcript when the ticket is closed.
- **Edited and deleted messages:** to help staff moderate, the bot keeps a log of
  edited and deleted messages in a staff-only channel. This can include the message
  text and, for a short time, a copy of image attachments so they can still be shown
  to staff after the original is gone.
- **Ticket and ban appeal details:** the information you provide when you open a ticket
  or file an appeal. A ban appeal may include an IP address that you choose to
  submit. The IP is used to check for a matching ban or flag; it is not retained in
  full, only a shortened form is kept in the appeal record.
- **Moderation records:** when staff take action (such as a ban, kick, or timeout),
  the bot records the affected user, the action, the reason, the staff member who
  acted, and the time. The bot also records changes to members' nicknames and
  display names so staff can review name history.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## 2. Why we process it, and our legal basis

We process this data to operate the server's moderation, ticket, and support
systems, to keep the community safe from spam and abuse, and to keep records of
moderation decisions so they can be reviewed.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in running and
protecting the community. An IP address you submit in a ban appeal is provided
voluntarily, as part of asking us to process that appeal.

## 3. Automated moderation

To keep the server safe, the bot automatically detects things like message spam,
disruptive pings, and prohibited words, and may remove messages or temporarily time
out an account without a human reviewing it first. Staff can review these actions,
and you can contact us if you believe one was a mistake.

## 4. Third parties

The bot runs on Discord, so your interactions are necessarily processed by Discord.
When you submit an IP address in a ban appeal, it is sent to a third-party IP
reputation service to check whether it is a VPN, proxy, or data center.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Open ticket data is
removed when the ticket is closed. Ticket transcripts, moderation records, and
edited and deleted message logs may be kept longer so decisions can be reviewed and
to help prevent ban evasion.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete
your personal data, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to lodge a
complaint with your local data protection authority. To make a request, contact us
at https://ddnet.org/staff/#contact.

Some records, such as moderation history, may be retained where we have a
legitimate need to keep them (for example, to enforce bans), even after a request.

## 7. Minimum age

The DDNet Discord server follows Discord's minimum age requirement (13, or higher
where local law requires it). The bot is not intended for anyone below that age.
